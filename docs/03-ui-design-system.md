# 1. Design Vision

JobMate should feel like a polished, trustworthy productivity product designed for people who are serious about their careers.

The visual experience should communicate:

- Professionalism
- Trust
- Simplicity
- Warmth
- Intelligence
- Confidence
- Reliability

The product should NOT look like:

- A generic AI chatbot
- A futuristic neon AI dashboard
- A college project
- An overly corporate HR portal
- A template copied from another SaaS product
